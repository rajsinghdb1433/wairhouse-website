## 2. What the big marketplace charges (the comparison that sells to owners)

Airbnb moved most hosts to a **single host-only service fee of about 15.5%** of the booking subtotal ([Hostfully summary](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/airbnb-host-fees/), confirm in the Airbnb host dashboard). The old model was roughly 3% from the host plus about 14% from the guest.

## 3. Recommended wAIRhouse pricing (already configured)

| Who | Fee | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Customer** | **6% service fee** on top of the price | Small, visible, and roughly a third of what travel marketplaces charge guests. Funds support, verification and payments. |
| **Owner** | **8% commission** from payout | About half of Airbnb's host fee, which is the headline for recruiting owners. |
| **Founding owners** | **0% commission for 6 months**, first **50 owners** | Solves the cold-start problem. Scarcity ("37 of 50 spots left", live counter on the site) drives sign-ups. |
| Listing | **Free** | No set-up or monthly fee. Removes all risk for an owner to try. |

Worked example, A$1,000 booking: customer pays **A$1,060**; owner receives **A$920** (A$1,000 in the founding period); wAIRhouse keeps **A$140** (14%), still under Airbnb's 15.5% all-in for the owner side alone.

Later options: a paid "Pro" plan (A$49 / month, 5% commission, multi-site dashboard, WMS/API integration), promoted listings, a cleaning/handling add-on marketplace, and insurance commission (partner with a goods-in-storage insurer).

| Real host example | $166 / month from one Melbourne parking space | [Yahoo Finance](https://au.finance.yahoo.com/news/easy-side-hustle-earning-zofia-1992-per-year-free-money-024001969.html) |

Competitor fees reported in the press: Spacer about 15% on top of the host price (2017 report); Parkhound about 23% commission. wAIRhouse charges owners 8% and customers 6%, and 0% for six months for the first 50 owners.

**Household-specific protections built in:** hosts confirm they own the space or have landlord, strata and council permission; the exact address is hidden until a booking is accepted; overnight stays and dangerous goods are banned; the Owner Agreement has a "Home and household spaces" section (permission, insurance, privacy, tax). Income is taxable, so hosts should keep records and check with the ATO or an accountant.

## 4. What a customer gets (the benefits list used on the site)

1. **No lock-in:** from 1 day to 2 years, extend or leave in a tap. Traditional operators typically want 3 to 12 months.
2. **No deposits or set-up fees.**
3. **Pay only for what you use:** by the pallet, m² or unit, instead of fixed unit sizes.
4. **Compare instantly:** price, features and map side by side, plus a 3D "will it fit" planner.
5. **Verified owners and reviewed listings;** reviews only from real bookings.
6. **Flexible access options:** 24/7 gates, forklift and dock access, cold rooms, and insurance-included listings.
7. **You are not charged until the owner accepts,** and a clear cancellation policy (full refund 7+ days out).

## 5. How to validate before launch

- Ring 10 warehouse owners in one industrial park (Eastern Creek, Moorebank, Dandenong South, Acacia Ridge) and ask: "If I sent you a booked pallet customer, what would you pay per pallet per week?" Adjust the price guide to the answers.
- Check the legal position on GST for the platform fee with your accountant.
- Check the 15.5% comparison on Airbnb's own help page before you use it in advertising.
